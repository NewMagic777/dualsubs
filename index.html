Dual Subs

Dual Subtitles for Language Learning

Watch Netflix with two subtitles at once and turn every episode into a language lesson. Fix, adjust and study the subtitles your way.

  • Two subtitle languages shown together
  • Edit subtitles: text, timing, split and merge
  • Word lookup and a word list linked to each scene
  • Shadowing: auto-pause at the end of each sentence, repeat lines
  • Every keyboard shortcut can be changed
  • Optional Google Drive sync in a private app-only folder
